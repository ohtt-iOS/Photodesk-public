  2. 계정 없음. 회원 가입이나 로그인이 없고, 이름·이메일 등 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다.

  4. 결제. 평생 무제한 구매와 복원은 Apple(App Store)이 처리합니다. 앱은 카드 정보나 결제 내역의 개인 식별 정보를 받지 않습니다.
  5. 기기에 저장하는 것. 정리 기록(보관한 사진 ID), 하루 무료 사용 장수, 광고 보상 시간, 사진 분석 캐시. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다.

  1. Photo access. The app reads your photo library to display photos, adds photos you choose to albums, and deletes only what you explicitly confirm. Photos and analysis results are processed and stored on your device only and are never sent to any server.

  3. Advertising. The app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads only when you tap the button to watch one. When an ad is requested, Google may process device information (advertising identifier, IP address, device model and OS, approximate location). Use of the advertising identifier follows your iPhone setting under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; if you decline, ads still show without personalization. Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies. Users in the EEA and UK can change their choices in the consent screen shown before ads.
  4. Purchases. The one-time "Unlimited Forever" purchase and restores are handled by Apple (App Store). The app never receives card details or personally identifying purchase data.
  5. Stored on your device. Sorting history (IDs of kept photos), today's free-photo count, ad reward time, and the photo analysis cache. Deleting the app deletes them.
  6. Notifications. Only local notifications scheduled on the device, and only if you allow them. There is no push server.
  7. Children. The app is not directed at children under 14.
